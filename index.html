brAInly Prototype Flow Instructions

This dashboard simulates client actions and maps them in real-time to your Cloud Firestore structure on the right side. Follow these steps for your live presentation demo:

  1. Click Get Started on the Welcome Screen.
  2. Click Continue as Demo User on the Login Screen.
  3. On the Home Dashboard, notice the greeting and progress elements. Click the Daily Routine card.
  4. Click Brush Teeth task, view the steps, and click Mark as Completed. Check the live updates in the Firestore Console (especially on the routineTasks and progressData collections).
  5. Open Learning Cards from the bottom navigation or Dashboard, select the Apple object card, solve the quiz, and observer progress.
  6. Open Social Scenarios, play the Greeting Others scenario, select Say hello back, and confirm success.
  7. View the updated scores on the My Progress dashboard page.
  8. View recommendations on the AI Suggestions page.
